# Trademarks

The code is free software under [`LICENSE`](LICENSE). The **Rillwood** name, the pebble logo and the app icon are not part of that licence: they identify the official app, so people know which build they're getting and where to report problems.

## Forks are welcome

Fork, change and publish Rillwood as the licence allows. If you distribute a build to others (an app store, a download, a TestFlight or Play test), please:

- **Rename it.** Use your own name, not "Rillwood" or something that could be mistaken for it.
- **Re-icon it.** Replace the app icon, splash mark and logo (`app/assets/images/`, `web/public/`).
- **Change the identifiers.** Your own bundle id / package (not `app.rillwood.client`) and URL scheme (not `rillwood://`), so your build can be installed next to the official one and its data stays separate.
- **Don't imply it's official** or endorsed by the Rillwood project.

You may say that your app is **based on Rillwood**, with a link to this repository. The licence and copyright notices stay as they are.

## Without asking

- Use the name to talk about the project: articles, reviews, tutorials, comparisons.
- Use it in an unmodified build for yourself or for testing.
- Link to the official app or this repository with the name and logo as they are.

Anything else, open an issue and ask.
