Downloads - one look for adults and kids

Today the Downloads tab and kids mode's Downloads page look like two apps: bare rows vs rounded cards, different poster and title sizes, caps captions vs bold section headers, a trash button vs ⋯. This draft keeps each page's own flow (kids lists every title the kid can see and downloads from it; the tab lists what's on the phone and plays it) and gives both the same parts. Dark palette, iPhone size.

  • 1 Downloads list
  • 2 A show's episodes
  • 3 What changes
One rowNo card behind it. 2:3 poster 56pt, or a 16:9 still 128pt for an episode. Title 15/600 (2 lines), then caption lines: size and state, then timer, Keeping 5 or Next. 12pt between rows.
One right-hand slotAlways 44pt, always at the end: ring while downloading (stop square / arrow when paused), round ↓ to download, round ✓ when it's here and the row doesn't play. A show row ends with ›.
One header styleSection headers 20/700 (Shows, Movies, Season 1) - the app's rule, no small caps. Page actions in ⋯, Delete All inside it.
Same topStorage bar, then the search field, on both pages (already shared). The tab keeps its type chips; kids keeps Shows / Movies sections.
Same words"1.9 GB", "Downloading 12% · 2 of 5", "Waiting for Wi-Fi", "Expires in 3 days". No "On this phone" - ✓ says it.
Same show pagePoster, name, "5 of 32 episodes · 1.4 GB", a bar while it downloads, season pills, then rows. The tab's show page gets the header and pills too.
