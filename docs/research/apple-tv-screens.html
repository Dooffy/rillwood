Rillwood for Apple TV - screen drafts

Design draft for a tvOS client (plan: docs/roadmap.md TV, spike notes: docs/research/apple-tv.md). Drawn at 1920 × 1080 in the app's dark palette, scaled to fit. Gradients stand in for artwork; every title is made up. Each screen shows where the focus sits.

A draft to build from, not final: details settle per screen task.

    Focus is the cursorFocused item lifts: scale 1.08-1.1, deeper shadow, light sheen. Buttons and rows turn white with black text. Nothing else shows focus.
    RemoteSwipe / click to move, click to select, Play/Pause anywhere plays the focused item, long-press for the item menu, Back (Menu) goes up one level and from a tab root to Home.
    NavigationGlass tab bar at the top: Home, Library, Search, Settings. It hides when you move down into content and returns on Back or swiping up.
    No downloads on TVtvOS gives apps no lasting storage, so the TV app streams only. Offline = a clear message, not a Downloads tab.
    Safe area90 pt sides, 60 pt top / bottom. Shelves bleed off the right edge so the next card peeks in. Text never sits on bright art without a fade.
    SizesPosters 250 × 375 (6 across), Continue Watching 548 × 308 (3 across), small landscape 399 × 224. Body 29 pt, captions 25 pt, titles 76 pt.
