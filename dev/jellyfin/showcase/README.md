# Showcase data

`showcase.json` holds the showcase library's metadata: titles, overviews, ratings, episodes, the demo user's watch state, and cast, directors, writers and studios. `art/` holds its artwork (terms in `art/README.md`).

## Everything is fictional

All titles, plots, people, characters and companies here are made up for demos and store screenshots. They don't describe real films, shows, persons or studios. Any match with a real name - a person, living or dead, a production company or a title - is coincidental and unintended.

Keep it that way when adding entries: invented names only, no real people, brands or works.
